# Keyword lookups for the next tools

28 November 2026. These are the lookups the research report left open in its section 7.

## Bottom line

- **Chart images: demand confirmed.** "graph maker" is 33,100 US searches a month, as the report assumed. "pie chart maker" is also 33,100 and "bar graph maker" 14,800, which the report did not have. I am building it.
- **Labels and name badges: search demand for the free page is much weaker than the report's score implied.** The phrases that describe the tool get 590 to 2,900 US searches a month each. Template phrases get 1,900 to 8,100. Only Avery's brand terms are large (27,100 to 135,000). I have not started it, as you asked. The options are at the end.

## Where the numbers come from

Every figure is a US monthly average read from a keyword page on seodata.dev, opened directly on 28 November 2026. The site states the numbers come "from the Google Ads API: the same numbers you'd see in Google Ads". Each page shows the date its data was fetched. The page for a phrase is `https://www.seodata.dev/keyword/` followed by the phrase with hyphens, for example [graph maker](https://www.seodata.dev/keyword/graph-maker).

Three limits:

- **One source.** I could not open Google Keyword Planner, which needs your Google Ads account. Semrush opened but hides its keyword tables behind sign-up, and Ahrefs and SpyFu did not load.
- **Google groups close variants.** "pie chart maker", "pie chart generator" and "pie graph maker" all show 33,100 because Google counts them as one. Do not add them up.
- **Nine phrases are not in that database**, so their volume is still unknown: labels from google sheets, google sheets labels, chart api, chart image api, quickchart, email countdown timer, countdown timer for email, email timer, linkedin carousel maker.

## Your decision on labels

The free page and API, without the add-on, would live on search. That search is small, and the largest phrases belong to Avery. Three ways forward:

1. **Build it as planned, aimed at the template phrases.** Pages for the common sheet sizes ("compatible with Avery 5160", with a no-affiliation line), name badge templates and place cards. Expect little traffic until the add-on exists. This is my recommendation if you still want labels: the build is 6 to 9 days and the page is the base the add-on needs anyway.
2. **Build it together with the Google Sheets add-on.** The add-on is where the measured demand is. It adds 8 to 12 days and Google's review, and I could not confirm that a developer in Lebanon can publish there.
3. **Skip labels for now and move the email signature generator up.** Its head phrase is 18,100 a month and a comparable seller has payment-verified revenue through Paddle. It is a 12 to 16 day build against free tools from HubSpot and CodeTwo.
