# Next tools for Link To Card: evidence, ranking and a homepage proposal

27 November 2026. Research and design proposal only. Nothing was built or deployed.

## Bottom line

**The evidence argues against the plan in one respect: a large catalogue of free tools does not pay.** Sites built that way earn almost nothing from their users, even with real traffic:

- 10015.io has 311 tools, five years of history and about 150,000 Google visits a month by its own listing. Its payment-verified direct revenue is **$2,755 in total**, about $46 a month ([TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/10015-io)).
- New in-browser PDF toolkits reach 5,000 to 21,000 visitors a month after six to nine months and earn **$0 to $205 a month** ([OxygenPDF](https://trustmrr.com/startup/oxygenpdf), [PDFWix](https://trustmrr.com/startup/pdfwix)).
- A builder with 112 browser PDF tools reported zero paying customers ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/112-browser-based-pdf-tools-0-paying-customers-what-would-you-do-4ecc43af2a)).

Free tools are worth adding only where the free page leads to a paid step that a business repeats. So I recommend adding three to five tools, not thirty.

**No idea came out "strong".** Ten are "possible" and score 20 to 27 out of 35. The verification round weakened seven of them and strengthened none. Treat a gap of one or two points as noise.

**The top three:**

1. **Label sheets and name badges from a spreadsheet**, with a Google Sheets add-on later (27/35).
2. **Email signature generator** (25/35).
3. **Chart images**: an API plus a free chart maker page (25/35).

**Fill and sign a PDF** also scores 25. It is fourth because its payment proof is the weakest of the three that tie.

**Two things outrank every new tool.** Checkout is still off in production until Paddle approves the domain, and the paid tools already built (PDFs, bulk documents, the PDF toolkit, email images) are still switched off there. Nothing in this report earns money before those change.

**The homepage you asked for is achievable on laptops and not on phones.** A wireframe measured in a real browser shows all 33 tools on the first screen at 1366×768 and larger. On a phone it shows 17 to 25 of 33 in a readable layout. None of the four sites you named shows all its tools on the first screen.

My confidence in the ranking is moderate for the top two and low for the order below them. The reasons are in "What I could not establish".

## How this was researched and what the labels mean

Ten researchers covered ten groups of ideas. Eight verifiers then tried to refute the key figures and fill gaps. I checked a sample of revenue pages myself.

Three kinds of page could be opened directly: TrustMRR startup pages, Cloudflare's documentation, and the four tool sites you named. Everything else was read from search-result summaries, because this environment cannot open other sites. That applies to every Semrush, Ahrefs and Similarweb figure and to most vendor prices.

Each figure in this report carries one of these labels:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **verified** | Revenue that TrustMRR computed from the seller's connected payment account, read on a page opened on 27 November 2026. An independent re-check of 20 such pages found every figure copied correctly. |
| **self-reported** | A founder's own statement in an interview, post or listing. |
| **estimate** | A third party's model: Semrush, Ahrefs, Similarweb, Latka and similar. Seen in a search summary unless stated. |
| **price** | A vendor's list price. Seen in a search summary unless stated. |
| **store** | A count shown by a marketplace, such as Google Workspace Marketplace installs. |

"Verified" has limits of its own ([TrustMRR FAQ](https://trustmrr.com/faq)):

- TrustMRR says its figures can differ from the provider's own by up to 30%.
- It sees only money that passes through the connected account, so ad income does not appear.
- A page freezes when the seller's key expires. Two pages used here are frozen: PostNitro since 18 September and VectoSolve since 24 November 2026.
- "Recurring revenue" is TrustMRR's monthly recurring revenue figure. It can sit well above or below the cash a seller actually received, so the tables also give 12-month revenue where it matters.

Two more limits apply throughout:

- **Keyword volumes are mostly missing.** The search tool rarely exposes them. Where a volume is given, it comes from a Semrush or Ahrefs page as shown in a summary, usually with no country stated. Where none was found, the report says "unknown".
- **Reddit and X could not be searched.** The search tool refuses reddit.com. Complaints therefore come from Trustpilot, G2, Capterra, Google Workspace Marketplace reviews and vendor forums.

## 1. What the evidence says about the strategy

### The case against many free tools

1. **Small multi-tool sites earn almost nothing from users (verified).** Besides 10015.io: heictojpg.fr has 130+ tool pages and $63 in total, with 361 visitors in 30 days ([TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/heictojpg)). OneClickPDF has 59 PDF tools and $8 in total ([TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/oneclickpdf)). terrific.tools has 800+ free tools and $2,420 in total, earned from a desktop app ([TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/terrific-tools-desktop)). A builder of 104 free tools reported zero paying customers ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/i-built-104-free-tools-solo-in-4-months-heres-the-honest-journey-so-far-671b2be76f)).
2. **The head terms belong to giants.** iLovePDF had about 216.8M visits in December 2025 and Smallpdf about 42.8M (estimates, [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/ilovepdf.com/overview/)). The subscription sites that win the commercial PDF terms buy them: pdfguru.com gets 77.84% of 12.05M visits from paid Google ads (estimate, [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/pdfguru.com/overview/)).
3. **Google sends fewer clicks and ranks new pages slowly.** Only 1.74% of new pages reached the top 10 within a year in an Ahrefs study of 2023 pages ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/how-long-does-it-take-to-rank/)). 68% of US Google searches ended without a click in early 2026 ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-zero-click-searches-reach-68-in-early-2026-study-479717)). Google's 2024 "scaled content abuse" policy targets many pages made mainly to rank ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2024/03/core-update-spam-policies)).
4. **Ads do not rescue it at small scale.** A Lebanon-based site with 1,500 calculators self-reports $1,450 of AdSense in ten months ([TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/thecalcs)).
5. **A free in-browser tool spends no credits.** It adds upkeep and no path into the plans.

### The case for a few free tools

1. **Narrow tools with a paid business step do earn (verified).** PDFBolt, a PDF API, has recurring revenue of $2,325 a month from 67 subscribers ([TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/pdfbolt)). Image-Charts has $6,072 a month from 48 ([TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/image-charts)). 1Lookup sells 76 data products on one shared credit balance and has $254,094 a month ([TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/1lookup)).
2. **The free page is the shop window and the API is the business.** CloudConvert says about 90% of its files arrive through its API (self-reported, [CloudConvert](https://cloudconvert.com/about)).
3. **Tool searches are less exposed to AI answers.** AI Overviews appeared on 2.1% of transactional keywords against 21.4% of informational ones ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-triggers/)).
4. **Serving free pages costs nothing.** Cloudflare does not bill requests to static assets ([Cloudflare](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/static-assets/billing-and-limitations/)).

### What follows

Each new tool should pass three tests:

- The free page does a real job, in the browser or with one watermarked render.
- The paid step is something a business repeats: bulk from a spreadsheet, an API or connector, no watermark, a hosted result.
- The search results are not owned by free giants.

One pricing fact shapes every low-volume tool. The Free plan gives 100 credits a month, and the $8 top-up is sold only to subscribers today. A tool that someone uses a few times a year can therefore only be sold as a feature of a paid plan, or through a top-up that non-subscribers can buy. Per-use credits alone will not charge them.

What the verification round changed:

- **Chart images.** The revenue figure held, but both incumbents sell unlimited charts. Our credits only win for small users.
- **QR tickets.** Paddle's prohibited list names "competition/event tickets" ([Paddle](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/what-am-i-not-allowed-to-sell-on-paddle)). - **Image to SVG.** The one verified seller uses AI tracing on a server and sells to crafters, who have no reason to buy cards or PDFs.
- **Carousel maker.** The revenue leader fell from $12,601 in February 2026 to about $3,000 to $4,000 a month by August.
- **PDF page tools.** The headline volumes were probably India or worldwide figures. The US figures look 8 to 14 times smaller.

## 4. The top three in detail

### 4.1 Labels and name badges from a spreadsheet

**Free pages and keywords.** One engine, a few pages, each with real presets:

- `/tools/label-maker`: address and product labels from a CSV or Excel file.
- `/tools/name-badge-maker`: badges and place cards.
- `/tools/qr-code-sheet` and `/tools/barcode-labels`: one code per row.

Target phrases: "print labels from Excel", "labels from Google Sheets", "name tag maker", "name badge template", "barcode label maker", "bulk QR code generator". **The volume of every one of these is unknown** and should be looked up before building. Pages for the most common sheet sizes would say "compatible with Avery 5160" and carry a no-affiliation line. Both large add-ons word it that way.

**The paid upgrade.** The free page handles up to 100 labels a run, text only. Foxy Labels uses the same cap. A paid balance unlocks unlimited labels, logos and images, a barcode or QR code per row, saved layouts, the API, and later the Google Sheets add-on.

**Suggested price.** 1 credit per sheet. The offer for this audience is the $8 top-up, bought once, with no licence to activate. That sits well under $39 a year. It requires one change: letting people without a subscription buy the top-up.

**Why it leads.** The complaints are specific and repeated, and we can fix them:

- Printed labels do not line up with the sheet ([Marketplace review](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/app/avery_label_merge/687713670524)).
- Paid lifetime licences fail to activate ([Labelmaker support group](https://groups.google.com/g/labelmaker)).
- Free limits are tight and unclear: reviewers cite 20, 30 and 50 labels.
- Avery's own tool is described as clunky and as losing saved templates after an update ([Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/www.averyproducts.com.au)).

**Main risks.**

- No label tool has a revenue figure anywhere. Willingness to pay rests on list prices and install counts.
- Avery's free tool and add-ons with millions of installs already serve the need.
- The Sheets add-on needs Google's OAuth verification. I could not confirm whether a developer in Lebanon can publish.
- Name badges must stay event name tags. Staff IDs, student IDs and press passes fall under your exclusion.

| gmail signature generator, outlook signature generator | unknown |

**The paid upgrade.** Remove the "made with" line, host the logo and photo, keep several signatures, make a whole team's signatures from a CSV, and add a banner slot drawn by the existing email images.

**Suggested price.** A feature of any paid plan. For people who refuse subscriptions, one $8 top-up unlocks a brand-free signature. Comparable one-time prices are $9 to $19. Team signatures cost 1 credit each.

**Why it ranks second.** It has the best pairing of sourced demand and verified payment. Both verified sellers use Paddle, so Paddle accepts the category. The complaints are about billing, not design. WiseStamp scores 2.5 out of 5 on Trustpilot over 118 reviews, for trouble cancelling and charges after cancelling ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/wisestamp.com?page=3)). Signature Hound ended its free service in June 2024.

**Main risks.**

- The search results are held by free tools from HubSpot and CodeTwo and by ten-year-old incumbents. Four recent entrants earned $4 to $759 in total.
- Conversion is low: SyncSignature has 137 payers among about 40,000 claimed users, about 0.3%, after three years.
- Hosted logos must stay online for as long as old emails exist, and they can be abused to fake another company's branding. Hosting should require an account.
- Signatures have to be tested by hand in Outlook, Gmail and Apple Mail.

### 4.3 Chart images

**Free pages and keywords.** `/tools/chart-maker` for bar, line and pie charts with a PNG download, plus a guide on putting a chart in an email. Sourced volumes: "graph maker" 33,100 and "chart maker" 8,100 a month. Old free sites hold both terms, and a new exact-match site got about 14,600 visits in three months (estimate, [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/website/graphmaker.ai/)). Expect little search traffic. The realistic channels are the connectors and the API documentation.

**The paid upgrade.** Charts by signed URL or API with no watermark, used in emails, PDF reports and chat messages. Charts inside a PDF made by Link To Card cost nothing extra, because the page is already charged.

**Suggested price.** 1 credit per new chart, with repeat views free. Starter at $9 undercuts the $40 and $89 entry prices for anyone sending under about 2,000 charts a month. Above about 10,000 a month a customer is better off at QuickChart, and we should accept that.

**Why it ranks third.** It has the best fit of any idea. It reuses the image pipeline, sells to the buyer we already have, and works in production today without waiting for PDFs. A test in Node drew charts with Apache ECharts and the bundled image library in 16 to 76 milliseconds. That still has to be repeated inside a Worker.

**Main risks.**

- The market is small. The verified leader has 48 subscriptions after nine years, and its monthly revenue swings between $2,311 and $18,673 with yearly renewals.
- QuickChart is free for modest use and can be self-hosted.
- Hot-linked chart URLs can be requested millions of times, so they need caps and caching.

## 5. Homepage redesign

### What the four reference sites do

I loaded each at 1440×900 and at phone size and measured it.

| Site | Tool cards fully visible on the first screen | Layout |
|---|---|---|
| iLovePDF | 10 of about 35 (1 on a phone) | Headline, category chips, then a 5-column grid of large cards. No search box. |
| Smallpdf | 0 | A hero first. Six "most popular" cards lower down, and a Tools menu in the header. |
| TinyWow | 0 | A search box and five category cards with tool counts. Tools appear below with category tabs. |
| 10015.io | 0 | A search box with a "/" shortcut. The tool grid starts about four screens down; the page is 39 screens tall. |

Three patterns are worth taking: iLovePDF's category chips, TinyWow's compact card with a category label, and 10015.io's search box with a keyboard shortcut. None of the four meets your "all tools on the first screen" rule, because they use large cards.

### The proposal

Use compact rows in category columns, not large cards. Each row has an icon, the name, a Free / Paid / Free + Paid label and a one-line description. This is the layout that iLovePDF uses inside its "All PDF tools" menu.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ▣ Link To Card                                                   Pricing   Docs   Sign in  [ Start free ]  │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Every tool, one click away.    [ ⌕ Search tools…                    / ]                                    │
│ (All 33) (Link previews 7) (Images 8) (PDF 12) (Automation 6) (Free only)                                  │
│                                                                                                          │
│ LINK PREVIEWS     7 │ IMAGES            8 │ PDF · FILES       6 │ PDF · DOCUMENTS   6 │ AUTOMATION      6 │
│ Open Graph check. F │ Share card build. ◐ │ Merge PDF         F │ HTML to PDF       ◐ │ API             P │
│ X card validator  F │ Card from a link  P │ Image to PDF      F │ Invoice generator F │ Signed URLs     P │
│ LinkedIn preview  F │ Email images      P │ Split, organise  F* │ Certificate gen.  F │ Zapier          P │
│ Slack preview     F │ Chart images     ◐* │ PDF to JPG       F* │ Bulk documents    P │ Make            P │
│ Discord preview   F │ Email countdown  ◐* │ Fill and sign    F* │ QR tickets       ◐* │ n8n             P │
│ WhatsApp preview  F │ Email signature  F* │ PDF toolkit API   P │ Labels, badges   ◐* │ Google Sheets  ◐* │
│ Site share audit ◐* │ Carousel maker   ◐* │                     │                     │                   │
│                     │ Image to SVG     ◐* │                     │                     │                   │
│                                                                                                          │
│ F = Free   P = Paid   ◐ = Free, with a paid upgrade   * = an idea from this report                         │
├───────────────────────── the first screen ends here on a 1366×768 laptop ─────────────────────────────────┤
│ Pricing in one row · how credits work · the card playground · FAQ · footer                                 │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The details:

- **Search box.** It filters the rows as you type and takes focus on "/". It matches names, descriptions and a few synonyms per tool.
- **Category chips.** All, Link previews, Images, PDF, Automation, plus "Free only". PDF is split into two columns, files and documents, so that no column is longer than eight rows.
- **One registry file.** The grid is generated at build time from one list of tools, using the site's existing feature switches. A tool appears only where its switch is on, so production never lists a tool that is off.
- **The current hero moves.** The card playground and the "how it works" section go to the share card tool's own page and below the fold.
- **Speed.** The grid is static HTML with no images. The filter is a few lines of script.

### Measured result

I built the wireframe as a static page and counted the rows fully visible on the first screen.

| Screen | Tools on the first screen | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1440×900 | 33 of 33 | Full names and descriptions |
| 1366×768 | 33 of 33 | Same |
| 1366×650, with browser bars | 33 of 33 | Rows tighten by 6 px |
| 1280×720 | 33 of 33 | Five names shorten |
| Tablet, 820×1180 | 33 of 33 | Three columns |
| Phone, 390×844 | 25 of 33 | Two columns, names and labels only |
| Phone, 390×664 with browser bars | 17 of 33 | The whole list is 1.7 screens long |
| Phone, dense variant | 33 of 33 | Three columns; 12 names are cut off and rows are 30 px high |

**On phones I recommend the readable layout, not the dense one.** The dense variant does show everything, but it cuts off a third of the names and its rows are too small to tap reliably. In the readable layout every category is one tap away through the chips, and the rest of the list is one short scroll.

| Standalone dynamic QR codes | Crowded: 16 sellers found, with a median near $22 a month; printed codes are a permanent obligation | One outlier has recurring revenue of $14,263 a month on AI-drawn codes, which this stack cannot do ([TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/qr-code-ai)) |
| Free public link shortener | No payers, and abuse lands on the main domain | Generic shorteners on TrustMRR earn $0 to $26 a month |
| Branded links with a custom preview on the customer's domain | The best fit of the redirect ideas, but the top verified seller has recurring revenue of about $490 a month and the feature is easy to abuse | [TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/flyn) |
| Redirect checker, favicon generator, meta tag generator, SERP preview | Real traffic, no payer found for any of them | favicon.io gets about 523,000 visits a month (estimate) and has no known paid plan |

| Background remover, upscaler, alt-text generator | Small sellers earn almost nothing; AI image tools draw payment-processor scrutiny | PixRemove $1 in total; Alt Text Pro $160 in total and for sale (verified, opened by me) |
| Website screenshot API | The earlier rejection stands | A Paddle-verified newcomer has $302 in total ([TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/boltshot)) |
| E-signature requests, uptime monitoring | The earlier rejections stand | SignWith $3,321 in total; UptimeObserver $1,950 in total after three years |
| Resume builder, business cards, letterheads | Revenue sits with one leader; small builders earn $16 to $470 a month | Rezi has recurring revenue of $229,471 a month ([TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/rezi)) |
| Product catalogs from a spreadsheet | People pay $19 to $99 a month, but the proven channel is the Shopify App Store, which forbids billing outside Shopify | Shopify's app requirements |

Three channel findings:

- **Google Workspace Marketplace** allows billing on your own site. Google's help says developers take payment directly.
- **Shopify and RapidAPI** bill buyers themselves, which conflicts with Paddle-only.
- **A Chrome extension** costs a $5 registration and may sell a service billed elsewhere. I found no evidence that one brings paying users to a comparable tool.

Two competitor notes:

- **opengraph.xyz**, the best-known free share-tag checker, now sells share images itself with a $59 lifetime deal.
- **Betterimage**, founded in August 2026, is a near-copy of this product's core: social cards, a preview checker, signed image URLs and an API. It shows no revenue yet ([TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/betterimage)).

One correction to my earlier report: it said the best payment-verified QR tool made $881 a month. That is out of date. QR Code AI has recurring revenue of $14,263 a month. The conclusion does not change, for the reason in the table.

## 7. What I could not establish

- **Most keyword volumes.** Unknown for every label, name-tag, ticket, raffle, barcode, countdown-timer, carousel and share-checker term, and for rotate, delete and extract PDF pages. These need a manual lookup in Google Keyword Planner.
- **The country behind the Semrush figures.** The summaries did not show it.
- **Revenue of any label tool, of Ticket Generator, of QuickChart, and of any share-tag monitoring product.**
- **Whether a developer in Lebanon can publish on the Google Workspace Marketplace**, and whether Paddle pays out to a seller in Lebanon. The second was already open.
- **Paddle's view of a ticket-making tool and of countdown timers.** Its list is "not exhaustive".
- **Reddit and X complaints.** The search tool cannot reach them.
- **Chart rendering inside a Worker.** It was tested in Node only.

One lead is worth an hour of checking. The only small PDF domains this research saw ranking do so in other languages: splitapdf.com on Arabic keywords in Saudi Arabia with about 136,000 visits a month, and createmysignature.com on Spanish ones (estimates, [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/splitapdf.com/overview/)). Arabic versions of the free pages may be where a new domain can rank. Nobody is shown paying there, and each figure is a single snapshot, so my confidence is low.

## 8. Decisions for you

1. **Which tools to build, if any.** I recommend labels and badges first, then the email signature generator. Chart images third only if you want a tool that works in production before PDFs are approved.
2. **The homepage direction.** Compact rows in category columns, with the readable layout on phones.
3. **Whether people without a subscription may buy the $8 top-up.** The label and signature tools depend on it.
4. **Whether to ask Paddle in writing about ticket-making tools.** Without that answer I would not build QR tickets.
5. **The keyword lookups in section 7**, before any build starts.
