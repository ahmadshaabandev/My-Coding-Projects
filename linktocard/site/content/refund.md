## 30-day refunds

If you bought something from [[site.name]] and you want your money back, ask within **30 days of the date of purchase** and you will get a full refund of that payment.

You do not have to give a reason. Changing your mind is enough, and so is the service not working the way the documentation says it does.

This applies to:

- subscription payments for the Starter and Pro plans, and
- top-ups.

## How to ask

Use whichever is easier:

- **Through Paddle.** Open the "View receipt" or manage link in your Paddle receipt email, or go to [paddle.net](https://paddle.net) and look up your order.
- **By email.** Write to <[[site.supportEmail]]> from the address on your account and say which payment you would like refunded.

Our orders are processed by Paddle, which is the Merchant of Record. Paddle issues the refund to the original payment method. How quickly the money shows up depends on Paddle and on your bank or card provider.

## Cancelling is separate

You can cancel a subscription at any time, from your dashboard or from the link in your Paddle receipt email. Cancelling stops future charges, and you keep your plan until the end of the period you have paid for. Cancelling does not by itself refund a payment; if you also want a refund, ask as described above.

## After 30 days

Requests made more than 30 days after purchase are decided case by case, and we try to be reasonable.

Nothing on this page limits any right to a refund or to withdraw from a purchase that the law of your country gives you.
