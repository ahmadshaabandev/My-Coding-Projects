    About the generator

    What it does, and what it does not.

    What it does

    • Multiplies each quantity by its unit price, adds the lines and applies one tax rate, in whole cents, and shows you every number before the PDF is made.
    • Prints exactly those numbers with the invoice starter template: the four facts a payer looks for at the top, your client and your business, the lines, the totals, payment terms and notes.
    • Leaves out every optional field you leave empty, so an invoice without a due date or a tax ID still looks finished.
    • Saves the invoice to your device as a PDF named after the invoice number. No copy stays with us.

    What it does not do

    • It does not know the tax rules or invoice requirements of your country. Check which details your invoices must show, and the rate you charge, with your accountant or tax authority.
    • It does not number invoices for you or keep a record of the ones you made. Keep your own copy of each PDF.
    • It does not send the invoice or collect a payment.
    • It keeps the “[[pdf.freeMark]]” line at the foot of each page. Invoices made through the API on a paid plan carry only your own branding.

    Only for real invoices

    Use it to bill for work you did or goods you sold, in your own name. Making a fake invoice or receipt, or one in someone else’s name, is fraud in most places and is forbidden by our Terms of service.

    When the API makes invoices for you, it works the same way: your system computes the amounts and sends them as text, and the template prints them. Nothing is calculated on our side.

    Do this via the API

    Make the same invoice from your own system with POST /v1/pdf and an API key from your dashboard. Send the numbers you computed; the answer is the URL of the stored PDF.

    curl [[site.url]]/v1/pdf \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ltc_your_api_key" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{

    "data": {
      "seller_name": "Fernhill Studio",
      "buyer_name": "Okafor & Lund Architects",
      "invoice_number": "INV-2026-0142",
      "issue_date": "Nov 25, 2026",

      ],
      "total": "2,400.00"
    },
    "filename": "invoice-INV-2026-0142.pdf"
  }'

    FAQ

    Questions about invoices.

    Short answers. Where the answer depends on your country, we say so.

    How does it work out the tax and the total?

    Each line is quantity times unit price, rounded to the cent. The subtotal is the sum of the lines. The tax is the subtotal times the rate you enter, rounded to the cent once, and the total is the subtotal plus the tax. Leave the rate empty and the invoice shows only the total.

    If you need different rates on different lines, or a tax that is calculated differently, work the numbers out in your own system and use the API, which prints the amounts you send.

    Which currencies and number formats can I use?

    Any currency: type its code or symbol in the currency field, and it is printed next to the amount due and the total. Amounts are written with a comma between thousands and a dot before the cents, such as 1,250.00. Type a dot for decimals: 1,5 is refused rather than guessed.

    Is the PDF a valid invoice where I live?

    We cannot tell you. What an invoice must contain (a tax number, a sequential number, the client’s address, a statement about reverse charge, and so on) depends on the country and on your business. The template has room for the common details; check the rest with your accountant or tax authority.

    Do you keep my invoices or my client’s details?

    No. The form is sent to our server once, made into a PDF and sent back to your browser; the PDF is not stored. We keep a count of PDFs per network for the current hour, and a record of the pages and the time each PDF took, without its content. Privacy policy

    How do I add my logo?

    Paste the address of the logo image as it is published on your website, starting with https://. It is downloaded once when the PDF is made and printed at the top left. The address must be public: an image behind a login cannot be downloaded.

    Is it free? Are there limits?

    Yes, without an account: up to [[pdf.toolPdfsPerHour]] PDFs per hour from one network, and after a few you may be asked to confirm you are a person. An invoice can have up to [[pdf.maxItems]] lines and [[pdf.maxPages]] pages. Every page carries the “[[pdf.freeMark]]” line.

    Can my invoices be made automatically, for example after each payment?

    Yes, with the API: your system sends the invoice data to POST /v1/pdf and gets the PDF back. The guide Invoice PDFs from a Stripe webhook walks through it with Stripe as the source of the payments, and Zapier and n8n can send the same request without code.
