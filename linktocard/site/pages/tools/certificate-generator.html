About the generator

What it does, and what it does not.

What it does

  • Lays out a landscape certificate with the certificate starter template: a coloured panel with your organisation and the title, the recipient’s name as the largest line, what it was for, and along the foot the date, the signature line and the certificate ID.
  • Prints an optional certificate ID, so you can look a certificate up in your own records when someone asks.
  • Hands you the certificate as a PDF download, named after the recipient. We do not keep a copy.

What it does not do

  • It does not sign the certificate in any legal or cryptographic sense. “Signed by” prints a name.
  • It does not host a page where others can check a certificate. If you need that, keep the IDs in your own system.
  • It leaves a small “[[pdf.freeMark]]” line along the bottom edge. Certificates made through the API on a paid plan have no such line.

You are the issuer

A certificate made here says that your organisation confirms something. Issue it only for a course, workshop or programme that you ran or are allowed to certify, in your own name.

Do not use it for diplomas, degrees, licences, official qualifications or any document of a school, university, company or government that did not issue it. Certificates that claim another issuer are forbidden by our Terms of service, and we suspend accounts and keys that make them.

Do this via the API

Run a course for many people? Make one certificate per participant from your own system with POST /v1/pdf and an API key from your dashboard.

curl [[site.url]]/v1/pdf \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ltc_your_api_key" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "template": "certificate",
    "data": {
      "title": "Certificate of Completion",
      "recipient_name": "Amara Nwosu",
      "achievement": "for completing the Advanced TypeScript workshop",
      "issuer_name": "Lanternworks Training",
      "date": "November 25, 2026",
      "certificate_id": "LW-2026-0381"
    },
    "filename": "certificate-LW-2026-0381.pdf"
  }'

FAQ

Questions about certificates.

Short answers, including what this is not for.

Which paper sizes can I choose?

A4, US Letter or A5, always in landscape. The design fills the whole page on all three; on this free tool the bottom edge keeps room for the footer line.

How long can the text be?

Each field can hold up to [[pdf.maxDataValueChars]] characters, but a certificate reads best with a short title and one or two lines about the achievement. It is always exactly one page: text too long for it is cut off rather than moved to a second page, so make a PDF and look at it before you send it.

Can I use my own design?

Not on this page. With an API key you can send your own HTML and CSS to POST /v1/pdf, or start from the certificate template and change it. Your own HTML and CSS

Can I make certificates for a whole class at once?

The page makes one PDF at a time, up to [[pdf.toolPdfsPerHour]] per hour. For a class, use the API: one request per participant, or one signed link per participant that you send by email.

Do you keep the names I enter?

No. The names travel to our server once, to be printed, and come back to you inside the PDF; nothing of the certificate is stored. What we do keep is a count of PDFs per network for the hour, and for each PDF its page count and printing time, with no names in it. Privacy policy

Can I make a diploma or an official certificate with it?

No. This is for certificates of completion that your own organisation issues. Diplomas, degrees, licences and other official documents come from the institution that awards them; making one in its name is forbidden here, and in most places it is a crime.
