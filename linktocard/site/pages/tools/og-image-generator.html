How it works

The AI draws the background. Your text is set on top.

From your choices to an image

  1. You choose a style, colours, a motif and where the text goes.
  2. Our server writes the image prompt from those choices only, in our own words. Your title, description, site name and page address are not part of it.
  3. Black Forest Labs’ FLUX.2 [klein] model, run by Cloudflare Workers AI, draws a background with a calm, empty area where the text will go.
  4. A second model looks at the background before you see it. If it finds readable text, a face, a person, a brand logo, a document or nudity, the background is drawn once more with a new seed, and refused if it fails again. Nothing is charged for a refused background.
  5. Our card renderer, the same one that draws every [[site.name]] card, puts a shade behind the text for contrast and sets your title, description and site name on the background, with your logo if you add one.

What you get

  • Free preview: [[ai.previewWidth]] × [[ai.previewHeight]] PNG with a small corner mark, kept for [[ai.previewHours]] hours. No account.
  • Full size: [[ai.coverWidth]] × [[ai.coverHeight]] PNG, made from the background you previewed, with the meta tags to paste: og:image, its width, height and alt text, twitter:card and twitter:image.
  • On the [[plan.free.name]] plan the full-size image carries the corner mark, like every image made on that plan. Starter and Pro images have no mark.
  • Many pages at once: bulk blog covers makes one cover for every post of a feed or a CSV file.

What is the free preview?

A [[ai.previewWidth]] × [[ai.previewHeight]] PNG with a small corner mark, made after a quick check that you are a person. From one network you can make [[ai.freePreviewsPerPageText]] per page a day, and at most [[ai.freePreviewsPerDay]] previews a day in all. It is kept for [[ai.previewHours]] hours, together with the full-size background behind it, so that the full-size image you then make shows the same background.

[[> ai-faq-credits]]
What is kept, and for how long?

A preview and the background behind it are kept for [[ai.previewHours]] hours and then deleted. A full-size cover is kept until you delete it or your account is deleted, because a cover named in your meta tags must not disappear. A logo you upload is kept for [[ai.uploadHours]] hours for the covers you make with it, unless you save it in a brand preset; then it is kept until you delete the preset or your account.
