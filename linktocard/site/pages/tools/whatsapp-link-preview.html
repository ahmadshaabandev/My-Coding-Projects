    FAQ

    Questions about WhatsApp previews.

    Short answers, and where we are not sure, we say so.

    Why does WhatsApp show no image for my link?

    The causes you can test are on the page or the image server: no og:image tag, a relative image URL, an image that needs a login or answers with an error, a slow server, or a heavy image file. The checklist above tests each of them. Keeping the image file small is a safe habit, even though we cannot quote a WhatsApp limit.

    I fixed my tags. Why does WhatsApp still show the old preview?

    Apps cache what they fetched for a link, and we could not confirm how long WhatsApp keeps a preview. A URL with a small change, such as ?v=2 on the end, is usually treated as a new link and fetched fresh.

    What image should I use?

    A [[size.og.width]] × [[size.og.height]] JPEG or PNG on an absolute https:// address, compressed as far as it still looks sharp, with text and faces kept away from the edges in case it is cropped. This is a safe general choice, not a WhatsApp rule.

    Is this a WhatsApp tool?

    No. It is an independent checker made by [[site.name]], not by WhatsApp or Meta, and the preview it draws is a mockup.

    Is it free? What does the checker keep?

    Yes, and you do not need an account. There is an hourly limit on fresh checks from one network, and after a few checks you may be asked to confirm you are a person. The checker keeps the parsed tags and the check results for [[inspect.cacheMinutes]] minutes, never the page's HTML. Privacy policy
