    What it checks

    • The og:title, og:description, og:image, og:url and og:type tags, the page's <title> and its meta description.
    • The image the tags point to: whether it loads without a login, its type, file size, pixel size and shape.

    FAQ

    Questions about LinkedIn previews.

    Short answers, and where we are not sure, we say so.

    Why does LinkedIn show no image for my link?

    The usual causes are on the page or the image server: no og:image tag, a relative image URL, an image that needs a login or answers with an error, or an image too small or too large. The checklist above tests each of them. These are the common causes, not LinkedIn's documented rules.

    I fixed my tags. Why does LinkedIn still show the old preview?

    Apps cache what they fetched for a link, and we do not know LinkedIn's cache time. A URL with a small change, such as ?v=2 on the end, is usually treated as a new link and fetched fresh.

    What image should I use?

    We cannot quote LinkedIn's own numbers, so here is the safe choice: a [[size.og.width]] × [[size.og.height]] PNG or JPEG on an absolute https:// address, well under [[inspect.imageWarnMb]] MB, with text and faces kept away from the edges in case it is cropped.

    Is this a LinkedIn tool?

    No. It is an independent checker made by [[site.name]], not by LinkedIn, and the preview it draws is a mockup.

    Is it free? What does the checker keep?

    Yes, and you do not need an account. There is an hourly limit on fresh checks from one network, and after a few checks you may be asked to confirm you are a person.
