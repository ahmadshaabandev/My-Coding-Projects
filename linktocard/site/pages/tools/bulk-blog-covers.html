How it works

One preset, a cover for every post.

Presets and posts

  • A brand preset holds a style, colours, a motif rule, the text layout, the size, and optionally a logo and reference images.
  • With the motif rule “auto”, our server picks a motif for each post from a fixed list of keywords found in its title. The title itself is not sent to the AI model.
  • Posts come from a feed, a CSV file, or a list sent to the API.

The job

  • The covers are made one by one in the background. You can close the page: the dashboard shows the progress, and we email you when the job has finished.
  • Each finished cover costs [[ai.creditsPerImageText]], taken when it is stored. When your credits run out, the job pauses and says so; it does not charge more than you have.
  • When it has finished, download every cover in one ZIP file.
  • From your own code, the same presets work with POST /v1/ai/batches, and a signed cover URL makes a cover the first time it is opened. AI images docs

Is there a free preview?

Yes, of one title with your preset: a [[ai.previewWidth]] × [[ai.previewHeight]] PNG with a small corner mark, after a quick check that you are a person, kept for [[ai.previewHours]] hours. It counts toward the [[ai.freePreviewsPerDay]] free previews a day from one network. The covers of a job are not free.

[[> ai-faq-credits]]
What is kept, and for how long?

The covers are kept until you delete them or your account is deleted. A preset, with its logo and reference images, is kept until you delete it or your account. The feed is read once. The titles and descriptions of a job are kept with the job.

How many covers can I make?

At most [[ai.maxImagesPerAccountPerDay]] AI images per account per UTC day, counting every background drawn, redrawn ones included. When the day’s spending limit for AI images on our side is reached, no new AI image is made until 00:00 UTC, and nothing is charged for the wait.
