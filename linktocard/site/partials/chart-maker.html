    Chart JSON
    Data

    One row per label, one column per series. To fill the table from a spreadsheet, copy the cells, with the series names in the first row, and paste them into any cell. Tab- and comma-separated text both work.

    Up to [[charts.maxSeries]] series and [[charts.maxPoints]] rows ([[charts.maxSlices]] for a pie or doughnut chart). Write numbers with a dot for decimals: 1250.5, or 1,250.5 with commas between thousands.

    Options
    Theme

    Prefix and suffix go around every number on the chart, such as $ or %. Stacked works for bar and area charts, horizontal for bar charts.
