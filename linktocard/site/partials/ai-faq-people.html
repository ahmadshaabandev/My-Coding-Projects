Can it draw people, faces or logos?

No. The prompt asks for no people, faces, hands, text, logos, documents, forms or screens, and before you see a picture a second AI model checks it for readable text, faces, people, brand logos, documents and nudity. A picture that fails is drawn once more with a new seed, and refused if it fails again. Nothing is charged for a refused picture. The check is good but not perfect: if you see something that should not be there, do not use the image, and tell us at [[site.supportEmail]].

There is no way to ask for a particular person, a face swap, an avatar or a likeness, a brand’s logo, or a document such as an ID card or a certificate. The rules are in the terms.
