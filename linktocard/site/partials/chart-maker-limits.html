What this tool does not do

  • It does not make interactive charts. The result is a still PNG image, with no hover, zoom or animation.
  • It is not a spreadsheet. It does not add up, sort or calculate: the chart shows the numbers in the table as they are.
  • The image carries a small [[site.host]] mark in one corner, like every image made on the [[plan.free.name]] plan.
  • Your data is sent to our server to draw the image, and is not stored. There is no account and nothing is counted; previews are limited to [[charts.toolRateLimit]] from one network.
  • Letters of some scripts, such as Chinese, Japanese, Korean or Arabic, are not in its fonts and show as empty boxes.
