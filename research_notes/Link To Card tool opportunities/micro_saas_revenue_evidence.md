# Proven revenue evidence for small developer and marketing tools adjacent to linktocard.com (research date 2026-11-26)

Method note: in this session, direct page fetches were blocked by the network allowlist for screenshotone.com, superframeworks.com, bannerbear.com and urlbox.com, so I stopped trying other hosts. Every figure below comes from web search results: page titles, URLs and quoted snippets. I did not open the full pages. Each figure has a verification label:
- **PAYMENT-VERIFIED**: synced from the payment provider, for example TrustMRR.
- **FOUNDER**: self-reported by the founder in a post, interview or dashboard.
- **OPEN DASH**: the company's own public metrics page.
- **SECONDARY**: an aggregator or blog repeating a founder claim.
- **ESTIMATE**: a third-party estimator such as Latka, Sacra or Levels.fyi, not company-reported.

"Undated" means the search result showed no date for the figure.

## Q1. Which solo or tiny-team tools in these categories publicly report revenue, and how much?

### Takeaway
Hard, repeated revenue evidence exists for three groups close to linktocard:
- **Screenshot APIs**: ScreenshotOne, about $32–33K MRR, solo.
- **Template image and PDF APIs**: Bannerbear about $50K+ MRR. APITemplate.io and CraftMyPDF about $15K MRR combined. PDFShift about $8.5K MRR.
- **Uptime and status-page tools**: Oh Dear about $1M ARR, Instatus about $40–50K MRR, StatusGator $17K MRR, Hyperping about $15K MRR.

Plain QR generators, URL shorteners and OG-only generators show only small numbers. Where the figures are payment-verified, they are mostly under $1K MRR.

**Verification context**
- TrustMRR says revenue is verified "by syncing directly with your payment provider using a read-only API key" — [TrustMRR FAQ](https://www.trustmrr.com/faq).
- A 2026 review lists the supported providers, including **Paddle**, alongside Stripe, LemonSqueezy, Polar and others — [ToolCenter review](https://www.toolcenter.ai/en/articles/trustmrr-review-2026).
- One dataset counts 12,948 public startups with payment-provider-verified revenue — [Crawlora](https://crawlora.net/datasets/trustmrr).

### Inferences
- **Closest proven fit for a solo developer on Cloudflare Workers.** Screenshot and URL-rendering APIs (ScreenshotOne, ApiFlash, Urlbox) and template image and PDF APIs (Bannerbear, APITemplate, CraftMyPDF, PDFShift) are the categories with repeated, multi-year, founder-reported revenue from tiny teams. They are also technically closest to what linktocard already renders.
- **Highest ceilings.** Monitoring and status-page tools (Oh Dear, Instatus, StatusGator, Hyperping) show the highest revenue among small teams. They are recurring "peace of mind" products rather than per-render usage.
- **Verified figures are small.** The only payment-verified numbers in these categories (TrustMRR) are all small: under ~$1.1K MRR. Large figures such as $32K, $50K and $1M ARR are founder-reported or secondary, so they are self-reported, not verified.
- **Bannerbear began as an OG-image tool.** Previewmojo reached only ~$400 MRR before Jon Yongfook broadened it into a general image API. That suggests OG-only is too narrow a product on its own.

### Gaps
- Full-page fetches were blocked, so I could not read exact dates, confirm figures in context, or check IH "Stripe-verified" badges on any Indie Hackers product page.
- No revenue found for:
  - Placid, Orshot and DocRaptor
  - Formspree, Basin and Formcarry
  - Distill.io, ChangeTower and changedetection.io
  - Certifier or any certificate maker
  - RealFaviconGenerator or any favicon tool
  - Any solo broken-link or SEO auditor
- Bannerbear's current MRR could not be confirmed from a first-party source. The open page now shows only selected metrics.
- Latka's Bannerbear estimate (~$991K, 596 customers, 2024) appeared in a search summary without a URL. I excluded it.
- Hyperping publishes a Baremetrics dashboard, but I saw no current figure from it.
- A Visualping self-reported statistic ("235,877 customers creating 1.47M monitors in the 12 months to Sept 2026") appeared in a search summary without an identifiable URL. I excluded it.

## Q2. Business sale prices and multiples (Acquire.com, Microns, Flippa) for these categories

### Takeaway
I found no completed sale price for a named screenshot, OG-image, QR, link or status-page tool in this period.

Category-wide benchmarks:
- Acquire.com's confirmed median SaaS profit multiple was 3.9x in both 2024 and 2025.
- A live Acquire listing for a PDF-generation and screenshot API asks 4.1x trailing profit, in line with the median.

### Cited Findings
- **Acquire.com median.** The median profit multiple for SaaS sales was 3.9x in both 2024 and 2025, with an average of 81 days on market — [Acquire.com Biannual Multiples Report, Jan 2026](https://blog.acquire.com/acquire-com-biannual-acquisition-multiples-report-jan-2026/).
- **By deal size.** Deals under $100K went for 3.7x and deals of $100K–$1M for 3.9x, measured as confirmed purchase price to net income — [Acquire Multiples Report 2025 (PDF, Feb 2026)](https://blog.acquire.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Acquire-Multiples-Report-2025.pdf).
- **2023 benchmark.** Deals averaged 4.3x TTM profit, ranging from 0.63x to 34x — [Acquire Biannual Multiples Report, Jan 2024 (PDF)](https://blog.acquire.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Acquire-Biannual-Multiples-Report-Jan-2024.pdf).
- **PDF and screenshot API listing.** "Highly profitable PDF Generation & Screenshot API SaaS" asks **$265K, which is 4.1x trailing profit on $65.4K TTM net profit**. This is an asking price, not a sale. Listing seen November 2026; its posting date was not visible — [Acquire.com listing](https://app.acquire.com/public/f5to5nqgj6-highly-profitable-pdf-generation-screenshot-api-saas).
- **Screenshot API listing.** Another screenshot API asks $2,000, with no revenue or profit shown. Asking price only — [Acquire.com listing](https://app.acquire.com/startup/o50jnuky7z-screenshot-api-to-quickly-and-easily-capture-high-quality-screenshots-of-any-website).
- **Asking prices across listings.** A 2026 analysis of 615 live Acquire.com listings found the average bootstrapped SaaS asks about 2.6x TTM revenue and 10.7x TTM profit. These are asks, not closes — [BigIdeasDB](https://bigideasdb.com/saas-valuation-multiples-2026).
- **Microns.io.** The homepage headline says a founder "sold a screenshot design tool for $6K". The listing and the date were not found — [Microns.io](https://www.microns.io/).
- **Flippa.** A free invoice-generator site monetised by AdSense asks $1,999, with no revenue or profit shown — [Flippa listing](https://flippa.com/12098023-free-invoice-generator-site-with-fully-customizable-templates-live-preview-adsense-ready-low-maintenance-high-rpm-niche-built-for-growth-and-scalability).
- **Bitly / Egoditor.** Bitly bought QR-Code-Generator.com's parent Egoditor for cash and stock (announced December 1, 2021). The price was not disclosed — [MarTech](https://martech.org/bitly-announces-acquisition-of-egoditor/).
- **TrustMRR marketplace.** TrustMRR also runs a marketplace of "MRR-verified" startups for sale — [TrustMRR startups directory](https://trustmrr.com/startups).

### Inferences
- **Indicative valuation.** A small profitable rendering or screenshot API sells at roughly 3.5–4.5x annual profit. At $65K of profit that is about $250K; the live listing sits right at that.
- **Free tools are worth little.** Ad-monetised free generators (invoice, screenshot design tool) sell for low four figures, around $2K–$6K.

### Gaps
- No closed-deal prices were found for named tools in these categories. Acquire.com hides most closed-deal details.
- The Acquire category breakdowns (for example "developer tools" or "API" multiples) and the churn-by-category data were not visible in search snippets.
- Microns and Flippa sold-listing details for QR, link-in-bio and status-page tools were not found.

## Q3. Price points that work for developers, marketers and no-code builders

### Takeaway
Proven tools in these categories cluster at:
- **Screenshot APIs:** $7–$29 entry tiers, about $79 at around 10K renders, and a free tier of about 100 renders a month.
- **Template image APIs:** $19–$49 entry tiers built on monthly credit allowances.
- **Monitoring and status tools:** $5–$15 entry tiers.

Credit or quota tiers dominate. Rollover credits and "no lock-in" are used as selling points.

### Cited Findings
- **Screenshot APIs, entry tiers.** Starter tiers cluster around $7–$10 per 1,000 screenshots: CaptureKit and ApiFlash give 1,000 for $7, SnapRender 2,000 for $9. Most offer about 100 free screenshots a month. These comparisons are vendor-written, from 2026 — [SnapRender pricing comparison](https://snap-render.com/blog/screenshot-api-pricing-compared); [Medium comparison](https://medium.com/@TheTechDude/screenshot-api-pricing-compared-what-you-actually-pay-per-screenshot-in-2026-18f38320251f).
- **Screenshot APIs, at about 10K a month.** One table lists ApiFlash at $35 and ScreenshotOne at $79. Another cites Screenshotlayer at $19.99 and SnapRender at $29. ScreenshotOne's starter is listed as $17 for 2,000 in one source and $29 in another (**conflict**) — [Rendex comparison](https://rendex.dev/compare/best-screenshot-apis-2026); [SnapAPI comparison](https://snapapi.pics/blog-best-screenshot-api-2026.html).
- **Bannerbear.** About $49 a month for 1,000 API credits (Automate), $149 a month for 10,000 (Scale), and Enterprise from $299 a month. A one-time 30-credit trial; no permanent free tier. Third-party, 2026 — [Templated.io comparison](https://templated.io/blog/best-image-apis-for-automation/); [Imejis pricing comparison](https://www.imejis.io/blogs/comparisons/image-generation-api-pricing-comparison).
- **Placid.** Four tiers with the same features: Basic $19 a month for 500 credits, Pro $39 for 2,500, Business $89 for 25,000 and VIP $249 for 100,000. Trial credits only; credits reported to roll over. Third-party, 2026 — [Imejis pricing comparison](https://www.imejis.io/blogs/comparisons/image-generation-api-pricing-comparison); [html2img comparison](https://html2img.com/articles/bannerbear-alternatives/).
- **APITemplate.io.** Entry price is given as $24, $29 (annual) or $35 a month depending on the source (**conflict**). Free plan: 50 renders a month — [Imejis APITemplate alternatives](https://www.imejis.io/blogs/comparisons/best-apitemplate-alternatives).
- **HeyFake.** Pro is $6.90 a month for unlimited API use, against ~$64 in revenue over the last 30 days. A cheap unlimited plan did not produce revenue — [TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/heyfake).
- **izoukhai.** The best-performing payment-verified QR tool pitches "no subscription lock-in": $881 MRR from 240 subscriptions, about $3.67 per subscription — [TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/izoukhai).
- **Form backends.** Formspree Personal costs $15 a month ($10 billed yearly) for 200 submissions, Basin $15 ($12.50 yearly) for 250, and Formcarry $6 ($5 yearly) for 500. The source is a competitor — [Splitforms comparison](https://splitforms.com/alternatives).
- **Change monitoring.**
  - Distill: paid from about $12–$15 a month (sources conflict).
  - ChangeTower: $12 a month for 25 pages, though another source says ~$110 a month (**conflict**).
  - changedetection.io: hosted plan $8.99 a month, and free to self-host.

  Source — [PageCrawl comparison](https://pagecrawl.io/blog/best-free-website-change-monitoring-tools); [Visualping: Distill alternatives](https://visualping.io/blog/distill-alternatives).
- **Uptime monitoring.**
  - Cronitor: $2 per monitor plus $5 per user.
  - UptimeRobot: its 50-monitor free tier has excluded commercial use since December 2024.
  - Hyperping: 20 free monitors, commercial use allowed.
  - Better Stack: 10 free monitors, commercial use allowed.

  The source is a competitor — [TellMeWhenDown comparison](https://tellmewhendown.com/compare/best-uptime-monitoring-for-indie-hackers).
- **Broken-link checker.** SecurityBot's checker is priced at $5 a month. The article is by SecurityBot's own founder, February 2026 — [SecurityBot blog](https://securitybot.dev/blog/best-automated-link-checker-tools).
- **Linktree.** Reportedly raised Pro from $9 to $15 a month in late 2025. Single secondary source, unverified — [ProductMint](https://productmint.com/linktree-statistics/).
- **Oh Dear.** Positions bundling against point tools: replacing it "with 5+ separate tools will be 10x more expensive" — [Indie Hackers case study](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/growing-a-web-monitoring-tool-to-1m-arr-by-building-in-public-X876kKI7Olsf4YBI8FWb).

### Inferences
- **Two price bands.** Developer-facing render APIs support $17–$79 a month tiers with ~1K–10K units. No-code and marketer-facing template tools support $19–$49 entry tiers. Monitoring "point tools" sit at $5–$15, and bundled monitoring (Oh Dear) commands more.
- **Price floor.** A screenshot or render API launched today faces a floor of about $7 per 1,000 renders from existing vendors.
- **Model.** Credits per month with tiers is the dominant model. Rollover credits (Placid) and "no lock-in" (izoukhai) are used as differentiators, which supports a top-up or credit-pack option alongside subscriptions.

### Gaps
- Vendor pricing pages could not be fetched, so all prices are from third-party or competitor comparisons and may be stale or wrong. Verify before use.
- No public data was found comparing conversion or revenue between usage-based and flat pricing for these tools.
- No data on credit top-up uptake was found.

## Q4. Distribution channels that worked, with evidence

### Takeaway
The repeated evidence points to:
- no-code marketplace integrations (Zapier, Make, n8n), credited by Bannerbear and pursued by ScreenshotOne
- long-tail SEO and free tools (MySignature)
- documentation and content (Bannerbear)
- building in public (Oh Dear, Instatus, Hyperping)
- a browser extension as the distribution base (T.LY URL shortener, Visualping)
- Product Hunt relaunches (Bannerbear)

I found no numeric attribution, for example "X% of customers came from Zapier", for any of these tools.

### Cited Findings

## Q5. Failure signals: crowded categories where many launch but few earn

### Takeaway
The clearest failure signals are:
- **Free or bare URL shorteners**, which tend to earn $0 and attract abuse.
- **Basic QR generators**: payment-verified indie QR tools mostly under $1K MRR, with Bitly owning the leading brand.
- **OG-image-only generators**: a free Vercel library covers the use case, and Bannerbear's OG predecessor stalled at ~$400 MRR.
- **Free ad-supported generator sites**, valued around $2K.

Screenshot APIs are proven but crowded, with a price floor of about $7 per 1,000 renders. The best-known solo player reports about 9% monthly revenue churn and a growth plateau.

### Cited Findings
- **Free URL shorteners.** One indie operator runs zip1.io, a free URL shortener that "costs me about $27/month and makes $0", and found a scam destination with ~90,000 clicks in 48 hours — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/i-run-a-free-url-shortener-on-the-side-someone-did-90k-scam-clicks-in-48-hours-d050aa2bd1).
- **Ad revenue.** In an HN thread, one commenter's shortener-like project earned about $40 in ads over a year; they concluded that ad revenue "doesn't work unless you're getting truly massive amounts of traffic" — [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=14718089).
- **QR tools.**
  - Payment-verified QR tools on TrustMRR: izoukhai $881 MRR, QR-Hero ~$510, QRtracer $65, StashKit $32 (shrinking) and DYQR $10, all snapshots seen November 2026 — [izoukhai](https://trustmrr.com/startup/izoukhai); [QR-Hero](https://trustmrr.com/startup/qr-hero); [QRtracer](https://trustmrr.com/startup/qrtracer); [StashKit](https://trustmrr.com/startup/stashkit-qr); [DYQR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/meathill-llc).
  - The category leader, QR-Code-Generator.com, is owned by Bitly since 2021 and was rebranded "QRCG by Bitly" on April 20, 2026 — [QRBench review](https://www.qrbench.com/reviews/qrcg-by-bitly/); [MarTech](https://martech.org/bitly-announces-acquisition-of-egoditor/).
- **OG images.**
  - Vercel's `@vercel/og` library (Satori + Resvg, HTML/CSS to PNG) generates social card images and ships with Next.js App Router projects. It is open source — [Vercel docs](https://vercel.com/docs/og-image-generation); [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@vercel/og).
  - Bannerbear's predecessor, an OG-image tool, reached only ~$400 MRR before the pivot to a broader API — [Bannerbear](https://www.bannerbear.com/journey-to-10k-mrr/).
  - Published "$3K–$15K MRR potential" figures for OG generators are explicitly "editorial estimates, not measurements" — [PlanMySaaS](https://www.planmysaas.com/ideas/micro-saas-ideas/og-image-generator).
- **Screenshot APIs.**
  - Crowding: many vendors with ~100-render free tiers and entry pricing of ~$7–$10 per 1,000 — [SnapRender](https://snap-render.com/blog/screenshot-api-pricing-compared); [Scrapfly comparison](https://scrapfly.io/blog/posts/what-is-the-best-screenshot-api).
  - Earnings outside the leaders: payment-verified small screenshot tools earn $64–$185 in 30 days — [HeyFake](https://trustmrr.com/startup/heyfake); [Screenshot Bro](https://trustmrr.com/startup/screenshot-bro).
  - Low resale value: one Acquire listing asks $2,000 with no revenue shown — [Acquire.com](https://app.acquire.com/startup/o50jnuky7z-screenshot-api-to-quickly-and-easily-capture-high-quality-screenshots-of-any-website).
- **ScreenshotOne plateau.** At $32K MRR, ScreenshotOne has 9% monthly revenue churn. With about $3K of new MRR a month, the analysis argues for a ceiling near $33K MRR — [Superframeworks case study](https://superframeworks.com/case-study/screenshotone).
- **Free generator sites.** A free AdSense-monetised invoice generator site is listed at $1,999 — [Flippa](https://flippa.com/12098023-free-invoice-generator-site-with-fully-customizable-templates-live-preview-adsense-ready-low-maintenance-high-rpm-niche-built-for-growth-and-scalability).
- **Free alternatives in other categories.** Form backends and change monitors face free or self-hosted rivals: changedetection.io is free to self-host, and form backends compete on 50-submission free tiers — [PageCrawl](https://pagecrawl.io/blog/best-free-website-change-monitoring-tools); [Splitforms](https://splitforms.com/alternatives).
- **Indie income baseline.** One 2026 guide says the median active indie hacker makes under $1K a month and the top 10% make $10K+ MRR. Its methodology is unclear — [BetterLaunch](https://www.betterlaunch.co/blog/indie-hacker).

### Inferences
- **What not to build.** Avoid standalone QR, shortener and OG-only tools unless they are bundled into something with a reason to pay: analytics, a brand domain, team workflow or templates. Dub reached seven figures by moving to link attribution and affiliate payouts rather than plain shortening — [TechCrunch on Dub](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/16/dub-co-is-an-open-source-url-shortener-and-link-attribution-engine-packed-into-one).
- **Churn on usage-based APIs.** Render APIs appear to churn heavily (ScreenshotOne's reported 9% monthly), so monitoring-style recurring products may give a steadier base.

### Gaps
- No systematic dataset on the share of launches in each category that reach revenue was found. All failure evidence is anecdotal or drawn from small samples.
- No shutdown announcements for named OG-image, status-page or form-backend startups were found.
