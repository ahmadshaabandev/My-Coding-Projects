# Texts with Quran in them, for measuring the Quran parts (tests/test_quran_parts.py).
#
# No Quran word is typed here: every placeholder is filled from the verbatim Tanzil files.
#
#   {s:a}        ayah a of surah s, in Simple-Clean words
#   {s:a-b}      ayat a to b of surah s, one after the other
#   {s:a:w-v}    words w to v (counted from 1) of ayah a
#   {u:s:a-b}    the same ayat, verbatim Uthmani lines (the mushaf script)
#   {n:s:a-b}    the ayat with their numbers after them: ... (1) ... (2)
#   {N:s:a-b}    the same with Arabic-Indic numbers in ornate brackets
#   {...!sub:k}  word k of the placeholder with one letter changed (an OCR-like misread:
#                ب/ت, ج/ح, د/ذ, ر/ز, س/ش, ص/ض, ط/ظ, ع/غ, ف/ق, ...)
#   {...!drop:k} word k left out
#   {...!hamza:k} word k without its hamza (أ إ آ -> ا, ؤ -> و, ئ -> ي)
#   \n           a line break inside one text
#
# Sections (the measured counts are in docs/CONTRACT.md):
#
# [lessons]      school paragraphs that quote ayat (Q2: a school text with Quran parts)
# [q1]           whole ayat and ayah ranges, with and without framing formulas, surah
#                labels and ayah numbers (Q1: the text is Quran)
# [partial]      partial ayat on their own (Q2)
# [misquoted_q2] lessons quoting an ayah with one OCR-like error (Q2)
# [misquoted_q1] whole ayat with one OCR-like error (Q1)
#
# One text per line; blank lines and lines starting with # are ignored.
